## Daily Maintenance Routine

### After Each Use

**Immediate Cleanup:**

1. Allow cooktop to cool completely
2. Remove spills while still warm (easier cleaning)
3. Wipe down surfaces with damp cloth
4. Replace burner caps and grates properly

**Safety Checks:**

- Ensure all controls are in OFF position
- Verify proper flame extinguishing
- Check for gas odors (call if detected)
- Clean up any grease immediately

### End of Day

**Final Inspection:**

- Visual check of all burner assemblies
- Ensure proper alignment of components
- Wipe control knobs and surfaces
- Check for any damage or wear

## Weekly Deep Cleaning

### Gas Cooktop Cleaning

**Burner Grates and Caps:**

1. Remove all grates and burner caps
2. Soak in warm soapy water for 15-20 minutes
3. Scrub with non-abrasive brush
4. Rinse thoroughly and dry completely
5. Check for cracks or damage before replacing

**Burner Bases:**

1. Clean around each burner base carefully
2. Use soft brush to clear any debris
3. Avoid getting water in gas ports
4. Wipe dry with clean cloth

**Control Panel:**

- Clean with mild soap solution
- Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasives
- Use soft cloth to prevent scratching
- Dry thoroughly to prevent water spots

### Electric/Induction Cooktop Care

**Glass Surface:**

1. Use cooktop cleaner designed for glass surfaces
2. Apply cleaner to cool surface
3. Let sit for recommended time
4. Scrub gently with plastic scraper if needed
5. Wipe clean with damp cloth
6. Polish with dry microfiber cloth

**Control Touch Points:**

- Clean with electronics-safe cleaner
- Use minimal moisture
- Avoid harsh scrubbing
- Ensure complete drying

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Gas Cooktop Problems

**Uneven Flames:**

- Clean burner ports with thin wire
- Check for proper cap alignment
- Verify adequate gas pressure
- Call for professional adjustment

**Yellow Flames:**

- Indicates incomplete combustion
- Check for adequate ventilation
- Clean burner assemblies thoroughly
- Schedule professional inspection

### Electric/Induction Issues

**Hot Spots on Glass:**

- Clean thoroughly with appropriate cleaner
- Check for cookware scratches
- Avoid dragging pots and pans
- Use proper cookware sizes

**Control Response Problems:**

- Clean touch controls carefully
- Check for moisture or residue
- Verify power supply stability
- Reset according to manual if needed
