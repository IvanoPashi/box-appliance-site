## Wolf Range Burner Problems: Expert Repair Guide for Orange County

Wolf range burners are engineered for precision and reliability, but even these professional-grade components can experience issues over time. Our Orange County Wolf specialists diagnose and repair burner problems daily, ensuring your range performs at peak efficiency.

## Common Wolf Burner Issues

### Uneven Flame Patterns

**Symptoms:**

- Yellow or orange flames instead of blue
- Flames that are too high or low
- Uneven flame distribution around burner
- Flickering or unstable flames

**Typical Causes:**

- Clogged burner ports
- Improper air-to-gas mixture
- Burner cap misalignment
- Gas pressure issues

### No Ignition

**Problems:**

- Burner won't light at all
- Gas flows but no spark
- Clicking but no ignition
- Delayed ignition after gas flow

**Common Reasons:**

- Faulty ignition electrode
- Clogged burner assembly
- Electrical connection issues
- Control valve problems

### Poor Heat Output

**Issues:**

- Burner produces weak flame
- Takes longer to heat cookware
- Inconsistent heat levels
- Cannot reach high temperatures

## Step-by-Step Repair Solutions

### Cleaning Clogged Burners

**Disassembly Process:**

1. Ensure range is completely cool
2. Remove grates and burner caps
3. Lift out burner heads carefully
4. Soak components in warm soapy water

**Port Cleaning:**

- Use thin wire or toothpick for ports
- Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasives
- Ensure all debris is removed
- Check for proper alignment

### Ignition System Repair

**Electrode Inspection:**

- Check for cracks or damage
- Clean with soft brush
- Verify proper gap distance
- Test electrical continuity
