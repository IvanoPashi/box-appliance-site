## Common Wolf Oven Issues and Solutions

### 1. Oven Not Heating Properly

**Symptoms:**

- Oven fails to reach set temperature
- Uneven heating throughout cavity
- Extended preheating times
- Complete heating failure

**Troubleshooting Steps:**

1. **Check Power Supply:**

   - Verify circuit breaker position
   - Test electrical connections
   - Confirm voltage requirements
   - Check for loose connections

2. **Inspect Heating Elements:**

   - Look for visible damage or breaks
   - Test element continuity
   - Check for proper mounting
   - Verify element resistance

3. **Temperature Sensor Testing:**
   - Check sensor positioning
   - Test sensor resistance
   - Verify wire connections
   - Calibrate if necessary

**Professional Solutions:**

- Element replacement service
- Sensor calibration
- Control board diagnosis
- Electrical system repair

### 2. Temperature Inconsistencies

**Common Problems:**

- Hot spots during baking

**Diagnostic Process:**

   - Check temperature stability

2. **Convection System Inspection:**

   - Test fan operation
   - Check air circulation
   - Inspect fan motor

**Orange County Service Solutions:**
