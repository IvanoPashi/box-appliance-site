## Daily Maintenance After Use

### Post-Cooking Routine

**Immediate Steps:**

1. Run grill on high for 10-15 minutes (burn-off)
2. Turn off gas supply at source
3. Allow complete cooling before covering
4. Quick wipe of exterior surfaces

**Safety Checks:**

- Verify all controls in OFF position
- Check for gas leaks (soapy water test)
- Ensure proper flame extinguishing
- Clear cooking area of debris

### Quick Cleaning

**Grates and Surfaces:**

- Brush grates while still warm
- Wipe drip pans if accessible
- Clean exterior with appropriate cleaner
- Check for immediate damage or wear

## Weekly Deep Cleaning

### Interior Cleaning

**Cooking Grates:**

1. Remove all grates and flavorizer bars
2. Soak in warm soapy water
3. Scrub with grill brush
4. Rinse and dry thoroughly
5. Season lightly with cooking oil

**Firebox Cleaning:**

- Remove grease and food debris
- Scrape interior surfaces gently
- Clean burner tubes carefully
- Check for spider webs or blockages

**Drip Pan Management:**

- Remove and empty drip pans
- Wash with degreasing soap
- Replace disposable liners
- Check drainage system

### Exterior Care

**Stainless Steel Surfaces:**

- Use stainless steel cleaner
- Follow grain direction when cleaning
- Apply protective polish
- Remove fingerprints and spots

**Control Panel:**

- Clean knobs and handles
- Check control functionality
- Lubricate moving parts
- Verify proper operation
