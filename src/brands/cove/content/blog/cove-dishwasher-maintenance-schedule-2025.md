## Daily Maintenance Tasks

### After Each Use

**Simple Daily Care:**

1. **Scrape Dishes Properly:**

   - Remove large food particles
   - Avoid excessive pre-rinsing
   - Load according to manufacturer guidelines
   - Face soiled surfaces toward spray arms

2. **Check Door Seals:**

   - Wipe down door gaskets
   - Remove food debris
   - Inspect for damage
   - Ensure proper door closure

3. **Interior Inspection:**
   - Check for visible debris
   - Ensure spray arms rotate freely
   - Verify proper rack positioning
   - Monitor cycle completion

### End-of-Day Routine

**Daily Completion Tasks:**

- Leave door slightly open for air circulation
- Check for error codes or unusual displays
- Verify proper drain completion
- Ensure detergent dispenser is closed

## Weekly Maintenance Schedule

### Filter System Care

**Weekly Filter Maintenance:**

1. **Remove Filter Assembly:**

   - Turn counterclockwise to remove
   - Lift out carefully
   - Inspect for damage
   - Note debris accumulation

2. **Cleaning Process:**

   - Rinse under hot water
   - Use soft brush for stubborn debris
   - Avoid harsh chemicals
   - Ensure complete debris removal

3. **Reinstallation:**
   - Verify proper seating
   - Turn clockwise to secure
   - Check for proper fit
   - Test operation

### Spray Arm Cleaning

**Weekly Spray Arm Service:**

1. **Upper Spray Arm:**

   - Remove according to model instructions
   - Rinse thoroughly under hot water
   - Clear holes with toothpick if needed
   - Inspect for damage or wear

2. **Lower Spray Arm:**

   - Lift out of position
   - Soak in warm water if heavily soiled
   - Clear all spray holes
   - Check for proper rotation

3. **Third-Level Spray (if equipped):**
   - Follow manufacturer removal process
   - Clean meticulously
   - Verify proper water flow
   - Ensure secure reinstallation

### Door and Seal Maintenance

**Weekly Door Care:**

- Clean door exterior with appropriate cleaner
- Wipe down interior door surfaces
- Inspect hinges for smooth operation
- Check seal integrity and cleanliness
