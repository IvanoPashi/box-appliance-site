## Common Cove Dishwasher Noises and Causes

### Grinding or Scraping Sounds

**Potential Causes:**

1. **Foreign Objects in Wash System:**

   - Broken glass or ceramic pieces
   - Utensil fragments
   - Food particles in pump
   - Debris in spray arms

2. **Worn Components:**
   - Pump impeller damage
   - Motor bearing wear
   - Spray arm wear
   - Filter system damage

**Professional Diagnosis:**

- Complete system inspection
- Foreign object removal
- Component wear assessment
- Replacement recommendations

### Squealing or Squeaking Noises

**Common Sources:**

1. **Motor and Pump Issues:**

   - Bearing lubrication needs
   - Belt wear (if equipped)
   - Motor mount problems
   - Pump seal issues

2. **Door and Rack Problems:**
   - Hinge lubrication needs
   - Rack roller wear
   - Door seal friction
   - Adjustment requirements

**Resolution Steps:**

- Professional lubrication service
- Component replacement
- Adjustment procedures
- Performance testing

### Rattling or Vibration Sounds

**Typical Causes:**

1. **Installation Issues:**

   - Improper leveling
   - Loose mounting hardware
   - Inadequate support
   - Cabinet resonance

2. **Internal Component Problems:**
   - Loose spray arms
   - Unsecured components
   - Worn shock absorbers
   - Pump mounting issues

**Professional Solutions:**

- Installation verification
- Component securing
- Vibration dampening
- Performance optimization

### Loud Water Sounds

**Water Noise Causes:**

1. **Plumbing Related:**

   - High water pressure
   - Water hammer effects
   - Pipe resonance

2. **Internal Water System:**
   - Spray arm blockages
   - Pump cavitation

   - Drain restrictions

**Orange County Water Considerations:**
