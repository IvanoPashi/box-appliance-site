### Water Supply Error Codes

#### E1 - Water Fill Error

**Problem:** Insufficient water entering dishwasher
**Possible Causes:**

- Closed water supply valve
- Clogged water inlet filter
- Faulty water inlet valve
- Low water pressure

**Troubleshooting Steps:**

1. Check water supply valve position
2. Verify adequate water pressure
3. Inspect inlet filter for debris
4. Test water inlet valve operation

**Professional Solution:**

- Water pressure testing
- Inlet valve replacement
- Filter cleaning service
- Water supply optimization

**Problem:** Water level detection malfunction
