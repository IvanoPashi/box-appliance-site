## Cove Dishwasher Maintenance Schedule: Keep Your Premium Appliance Running Perfectly

Proper maintenance is crucial for preserving the performance and longevity of your Cove dishwasher. This comprehensive maintenance schedule will help you keep your premium appliance running at peak efficiency.

## Daily Maintenance Tasks

### After Each Use

- **Scrape Dishes**: Remove food debris before loading
- **Check Filters**: Ensure bottom filter is clear
- **Wipe Door Seals**: Clean with damp cloth
- **Leave Door Ajar**: Allow air circulation to prevent odors

## Weekly Maintenance

### Filter Cleaning

1. Remove bottom dish rack
2. Unscrew cylindrical filter
3. Rinse under hot water
4. Use soft brush for stubborn debris
5. Reinstall securely

### Spray Arm Inspection

- Check for clogs in spray arm holes
- Remove and rinse if necessary
- Ensure proper rotation

## Monthly Deep Cleaning

### Interior Cleaning

1. Run empty cycle with dishwasher cleaner
2. Wipe interior surfaces
3. Clean door seals and gaskets
4. Check and clean detergent dispenser

### Exterior Care

- Clean stainless steel surfaces
- Check control panel operation
- Inspect door alignment

## Quarterly Professional Services

### Recommended Professional Maintenance

- **Water Quality Testing**: Check for hard water effects
- **Performance Calibration**: Optimize wash cycles
- **Electrical Inspection**: Ensure safe operation
- **Seal and Gasket Inspection**: Prevent leaks

## Annual Comprehensive Service

### Professional Annual Maintenance

- Complete system diagnostic
- Pump and motor inspection
- Control board testing
- Replacement of wear components

## Signs Your Cove Dishwasher Needs Professional Attention

1. **Cleaning Issues**: Dishes remain dirty after full cycle
2. **Drainage Problems**: Standing water or slow drainage
3. **Unusual Noises**: Grinding, rattling, or humming sounds
4. **Electrical Issues**: Control panel malfunctions
5. **Leaking**: Water around door or base
