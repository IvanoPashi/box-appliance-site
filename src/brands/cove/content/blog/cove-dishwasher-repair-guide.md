## Cove Dishwasher Repair Guide: Common Problems and Solutions

Cove dishwashers represent the pinnacle of premium dishwashing technology. When these sophisticated appliances experience issues, proper diagnosis and repair are essential to restore optimal performance.

## Common Cove Dishwasher Problems

### 1. Poor Cleaning Performance
- **Symptoms**: Dishes remain dirty, soap residue, spots on glassware
- **Causes**: Clogged spray arms, worn wash pumps, incorrect detergent
- **Solutions**: Deep cleaning, pump repair, detergent adjustment

### 2. Drainage Issues
- **Signs**: Standing water, slow drainage, gurgling sounds
- **Causes**: Blocked filters, clogged drain hose, garbage disposal issues
- **Fixes**: Filter cleaning, drain line service, disposal repair

### 3. Noise Problems
- **Issues**: Grinding, humming, or rattling sounds
- **Causes**: Worn bearings, loose components, foreign objects
- **Repair**: Component replacement, debris removal

## Maintenance Tips for Cove Dishwashers

1. **Monthly Deep Cleaning**: Run empty cycle with dishwasher cleaner
2. **Filter Maintenance**: Clean filters weekly
3. **Spray Arm Care**: Remove and clean spray arms monthly
4. **Water Quality**: Use water softener if needed
