When your Sub-Zero refrigerator stops cooling properly, it can be both frustrating and costly if food spoils. ## Common Causes of Sub-Zero Cooling Problems

### 1. Temperature Settings Issues
**Check first:** Ensure temperature settings haven't been accidentally changed
- **Refrigerator compartment:** Should be set between 37-40°F
- **Freezer compartment:** Should be set between 0-5°F
- **Solution:** Adjust settings and wait 24 hours for stabilization

### 2. Dirty Condenser Coils
**Most common cause:** Dust buildup reduces cooling efficiency
- **Location:** Behind or underneath the unit
- **Signs:** Unit runs constantly, warm exterior
- **DIY Fix:** Vacuum coils every 6 months using brush attachment

### 3. Blocked Air Vents
**Easy fix:** Obstructed airflow prevents proper circulation
- **Check:** Interior vents not blocked by food items
- **Solution:** Reorganize contents to ensure 1-2 inches clearance around vents

### 4. Door Seal Problems
**Common in older units:** Damaged gaskets allow warm air infiltration
- **Test:** Dollar bill test - close door on bill, should require force to remove
- **Signs:** Condensation, frost buildup, increased energy bills
- **Professional repair needed:** Seal replacement requires expertise
