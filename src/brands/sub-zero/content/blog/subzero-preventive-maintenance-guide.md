## Monthly Maintenance Tasks

### Interior Cleaning
**Refrigerator compartment:**
- Remove all food items
- Clean shelves and drawers with mild soap
- Wipe interior walls and ceiling
- Clean door gaskets and hinges

**Freezer compartment:**
- Defrost if ice buildup exceeds 1/4 inch
- Clean ice storage bins
- Wipe freezer walls and shelves
- Check door seals for damage

### Exterior Care
- Clean stainless steel surfaces with appropriate cleaner
- Wipe control panels with soft, damp cloth
- Clean door handles and dispenser area
- Check for loose or damaged components

## Quarterly Maintenance

### Water System Service
**Filter replacement:**
- Replace water filter every 3-6 months
- Flush system after filter change
- Check for leaks around filter housing
- Test water taste and quality

**Ice maker maintenance:**
- Clean ice storage bin thoroughly
- Inspect ice maker components
- Test ice quality and production rate
- Clean water lines if accessible

### Condenser Coil Cleaning
**Critical for efficiency:**
- Locate condenser coils (usually rear or bottom)
- Vacuum dust and debris buildup
- Use coil cleaning brush for stubborn dirt
- Check condenser fan operation
