## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Check Basic Settings

**Start with these simple checks:**
- Ensure ice maker is turned ON (switch or wire arm position)
- Verify water supply valve is open
- Check that ice bin is properly positioned
- Confirm refrigerator temperature is correct (0°F freezer, 38°F refrigerator)

### 2. Inspect Water Filter

**A clogged water filter is the #1 cause of ice maker problems:**
- Replace water filter every 6 months
- If filter is old, water flow will be restricted
- Look for reduced water pressure in dispenser

**{{ site.appliance_brand }} water filter locations:**
- Built-in models: Usually in upper right corner of refrigerator
- Some models: Behind the grille at the bottom
- Professional series: Check owner's manual for specific location

### 3. Water Line Issues

**Check for water supply problems:**
- Ensure refrigerator is properly connected to water line
- Look for kinked or frozen water lines
- Verify household water pressure (should be 20-120 PSI)
- Check for water line shut-off valve position

### 4. Ice Maker Module Testing

**{{ site.appliance_brand }}-specific diagnostic steps:**
- Locate the test button on your ice maker
- Press and hold for 5-10 seconds
- Listen for motor sounds and water filling
- Observe complete cycle (takes about 6 minutes)
