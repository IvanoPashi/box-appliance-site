## Common Temperature Control Symptoms

### Refrigerator Compartment Too Warm

**Warning Signs:**

- Food spoiling faster than normal
- Temperature consistently above 38°F
- Milk and beverages warm
- Excessive condensation inside

**Potential Causes:**

- Faulty temperature sensor
- Control board malfunction
- Compressor issues
- Refrigerant problems

### Freezer Section Too Warm

**Symptoms:**

- Ice cream soft or melting
- Frozen foods thawing
- Ice buildup or frost
- Temperature above 0°F

**Common Issues:**

- Defrost system problems
- Evaporator fan failure
- Sealed system leaks
- Control sensor failure

### Temperature Fluctuations

- Inconsistent temperatures throughout day
- Different zones at different temperatures
- Temperature swings of 10°F or more
- Food quality varying unpredictably

## Step-by-Step Diagnosis

### 1. Check Temperature Settings

**Optimal settings:**

- **Refrigerator**: 37-38°F
- **Freezer**: 0°F
- **Wine zones**: 45-55°F depending on type

### 2. Test Temperature Accuracy

**Use an independent thermometer:**

- Place in center of refrigerator for 24 hours
- Compare reading to display temperature
- Check multiple locations within unit
- Document temperature over several days

### 3. Inspect Door Seals

**Poor seals cause temperature problems:**

- Check for tears or warping
- Test seal by closing door on dollar bill
- Look for light leaks around door edges
- Clean seals with mild soap solution

### 4. Examine Air Circulation

**Blocked airflow affects temperature:**

- Ensure vents aren't blocked by food
- Check for ice buildup on evaporator
- Verify fans are running smoothly
- Remove excessive frost or ice

## Common Causes and Solutions

### Faulty Temperature Sensor

### Refrigerant Issues
