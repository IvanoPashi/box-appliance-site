## Signs Your {{ site.appliance_brand }} Door Seal Needs Replacement

### 1. Visual Inspection Signs

**Look for these obvious problems:**
- **Cracks or tears** in the rubber gasket
- **Warping or deformation** of the seal surface
- **Discoloration** from age or contamination
- **Mold or mildew** growth on the seal
- **Missing pieces** or damaged sections

### 2. Performance Issues

**Temperature and efficiency problems:**
- **Higher energy bills** without explanation
- **Temperature fluctuations** inside appliance
- **Excessive frost buildup** in freezer sections
- **Condensation** on exterior of door
- **Food spoiling faster** than normal

### 3. Physical Tests

**Simple tests you can perform:**

#### Dollar Bill Test
1. Place a dollar bill in the door seal
2. Close the door completely
3. Try to pull the bill out
4. If it slides out easily, seal needs replacement

#### Flashlight Test
1. Shine flashlight inside closed appliance
2. Look for light leaks around door perimeter
3. Any visible light indicates seal failure

#### Touch Test
1. Run your hand around closed door perimeter
2. Feel for air movement or temperature differences
3. Cold or warm spots indicate seal problems
