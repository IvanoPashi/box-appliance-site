## Step-by-Step Filter Replacement Guide

### Tools Required:

- New Sub-Zero water filter (model-specific)
- Clean towel or cloth
- Flashlight (if needed for visibility)

### Replacement Process:

#### Step 1: Locate Your Filter

**Built-in Models:**

- Usually inside refrigerator compartment
- Upper right or left corner
- Behind removable cover panel

**Standalone Models:**

- May be in base grille area
- Check owner's manual for specific location

#### Step 2: Remove Old Filter

1. **Turn off ice maker:** Prevent water flow during replacement
2. **Turn filter counterclockwise:** Usually 1/4 turn
3. **Pull straight out:** Remove old filter carefully
4. **Check for leaks:** Inspect connections and housing

#### Step 3: Install New Filter

1. **Remove protective caps:** From new filter ends
2. **Insert new filter:** Push firmly into housing
3. **Turn clockwise:** Secure with 1/4 turn
4. **Replace cover panel:** Ensure proper seating

#### Step 4: System Activation

1. **Turn ice maker back on:** Restore normal operation
2. **Reset filter indicator:** Press and hold reset button
3. **Run water dispenser:** 5 minutes to clear air and carbon dust
4. **Check for leaks:** Inspect all connections

## Troubleshooting Common Filter Issues

### Filter Won't Fit

**Possible Causes:**

- Wrong filter model
- Old filter remnants in housing
- Damaged housing threads

**Solutions:**

- Verify correct part number
- Clean housing thoroughly
- Contact professional for housing repair

### Water Flow Problems

**Symptoms:**

- Slow water dispensing
- No water flow
- Gurgling sounds

**Troubleshooting:**

- Check filter installation
- Look for air locks in system
- Verify water supply pressure
- Prime the system properly

### Taste or Odor Issues

**Common Problems:**

- Chlorine taste persists
- Musty or stale odors
- Metallic taste

**Solutions:**

- Run additional water to flush system
- Check filter expiration date
- Verify genuine Sub-Zero filter
- Test water source quality
