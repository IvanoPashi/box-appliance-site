## Most Common Sub-Zero Ice Maker Issues

### 1. No Ice Production
**Symptoms:** Ice maker stops making ice completely
**Common Causes:**
- Water supply issues
- Clogged water filter
- Frozen water lines
- Control panel settings
- Faulty ice maker module

### 2. Low Ice Production
**Symptoms:** Ice maker produces less ice than normal
**Possible Reasons:**
- Partially clogged filter
- Low water pressure
- High ambient temperature
- Dirty condenser coils
- Door seal problems allowing warm air

### 3. Poor Ice Quality
**Issues Include:**
- Cloudy or white ice
- Small, hollow cubes
- Ice with unusual taste or odor
- Soft or mushy ice

### 4. Ice Maker Stuck or Jammed
**Signs:**
- Ice cubes stuck in maker
- Unusual grinding noises
- Harvest cycle not completing
- Ice backing up in chute

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Guide

### Initial Checks (Do These First)

#### Power and Settings Verification
1. **Check power:** Ensure ice maker is ON
2. **Verify settings:** Confirm ice production is enabled
3. **Test switch:** Toggle ice maker switch off/on
4. **Wait period:** Allow 24 hours for full cycle

#### Water Supply Assessment  
1. **Water filter:** Check if filter needs replacement (every 6 months)
2. **Water line:** Inspect for kinks or frozen sections
3. **Water pressure:** Should be 20-120 psi for optimal operation
4. **Supply valve:** Ensure water shut-off valve is fully open

### Advanced Diagnostic Steps

#### Temperature and Environment
- **Freezer temperature:** Must be 0-5°F for proper ice production
- **Room temperature:** High ambient temperatures affect performance
- **Door seals:** Check for air leaks affecting internal temperature

#### Mechanical Components
- **Harvest arm:** Should move freely without obstruction
- **Water inlet valve:** Listen for valve activation during fill cycle
- **Ice mold:** Inspect for damage or mineral buildup

## When Professional Sub-Zero Repair is Needed

- **Control board failures:** Digital display errors, unresponsive controls

### Mechanical Repairs
- **Motor replacement:** Harvest motor or gear motor failures
- **Valve repairs:** Water inlet valve or drain valve issues
- **Module replacement:** Complete ice maker module failure

### Refrigeration System
- **Refrigerant issues:** Low refrigerant affecting ice formation
- **Evaporator problems:** Frozen or damaged evaporator coils
- **Compressor issues:** Affecting overall cooling performance
