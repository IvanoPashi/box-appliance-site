## Monthly Maintenance Tasks

### Interior Cleaning (Every 2-4 Weeks)

**Refrigerator Compartment:**
1. Remove all food items and shelves
2. Mix warm water with mild soap (avoid harsh chemicals)
3. Clean all surfaces with a soft cloth
4. Pay special attention to door seals and gaskets
5. Dry thoroughly before replacing items
6. Check expiration dates while reorganizing

**Freezer Compartment:**
1. Remove frozen items (use cooler if needed)
2. Allow frost buildup to melt naturally
3. Clean with baking soda solution (2 tbsp per quart water)
4. Wipe dry and ensure proper airflow around items
5. Check door seals for ice buildup

**Wine Cooler (if applicable):**
1. Remove wine bottles carefully
2. Clean glass shelves with glass cleaner
3. Wipe interior with mild soap solution
4. Check humidity levels and adjust if needed
5. Inspect cork integrity on opened bottles

### Exterior Care

**Stainless Steel Surfaces:**
1. Use stainless steel cleaner or mild soap
2. Clean in direction of grain (usually vertical)
3. Buff dry with microfiber cloth
4. Apply stainless steel polish monthly

**Control Panels:**
1. Clean with slightly damp cloth
2. Avoid getting moisture in button gaps
3. Test all functions after cleaning

## Quarterly Maintenance (Every 3 Months)

### Deep Cleaning Tasks

**Condenser Coils:**
- **Location**: Usually behind or underneath unit
- **Process**: Vacuum gently with brush attachment
- **Importance**: Dirty coils force compressor to work harder
- **Orange County Note**: More frequent cleaning needed during Santa Ana wind season

**Door Seals and Gaskets:**
1. Inspect for cracks, tears, or warping
2. Clean with warm soapy water
3. Check magnetic seal strength with dollar bill test
4. Apply thin layer of petroleum jelly if recommended by model

**Drainage System:**
1. Locate drain pan and drip tray
2. Remove and wash with bleach solution
3. Check drain lines for clogs
4. Ensure proper drainage flow

**Temperature Calibration:**
1. Use appliance thermometer to verify settings
2. Refrigerator should maintain 37-38°F
3. Freezer should maintain 0-5°F
4. Wine cooler varies by wine type (45-65°F)

### Filter Maintenance

**Water Filter Replacement:**
- **Frequency**: Every 6 months or per indicator light
- **Signs for Replacement**: Slow water flow, bad taste, indicator light
- **Process**: Follow model-specific instructions
- **Orange County Water**: May need more frequent changes due to hard water

**Air Filter (if equipped):**
- Check every 3 months
- Replace if discolored or clogged
- Important for models with air purification systems

## Warning Signs: When to Call Professionals

### Immediate Service Required
- Temperature fluctuations beyond normal range
- Unusual noises (grinding, clicking, buzzing)
- Water leaks or puddles
- Ice buildup in refrigerator section
- Electrical issues or error codes

### Schedule Service Soon
- Doors not sealing properly
- Higher than normal energy bills
- Inconsistent ice production
- Strange odors that cleaning doesn't resolve
- Visible wear on gaskets or seals
